A note for you

My Princess Sarika,

I know Diwali feels different when you are this far from Mom, Dad, Palak and Kairu. No crackers echoing down the street, no Mom's dal on the table, no whole family talking at once.

Your home is about 13,000 km away, but you have already found a home in Auckland, and that home is me. I made you this little corner to say it again: you are not doing this Diwali alone, and I will always be with you.

Rajasthan gave you your roots. Delhi gave you your city, your chai and your chaat. You carry both with you, all the way across the world, and that is one of my favourite things about you.

Happy Diwali, my Princess.

I love you the most, my love

Shahi farmaan

A royal decree

शाही फरमान

Be it known across this home that on Diwali, the Princess of Rajasthan shall:

  1. Choose the first mithai (ghevar, obviously), and the biggest piece.
  2. Control the music, and no song may be skipped.
  3. Wash not a single dish.
  4. Be told at least once an hour that she is loved.
  5. Feel at home, wherever home happens to be.

Signed with love, your loyal subject
